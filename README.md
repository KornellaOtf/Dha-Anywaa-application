This is an app that helps people who are interested in learning to read and
speak the Anywaa language — and much more.

## The Anywaa language app

Dha Anywaa (also written Anuak) is a Nilo-Saharan language of the Eastern
Sudanic branch, closely related to Dinka and Nuer, spoken primarily in the
Gambella region of Ethiopia and along the rivers of South Sudan. It has its
own alphabet and unique rules.

This app was created to help anyone — absolute beginners and fluent speakers
alike — learn the language. It is user-friendly, featuring pictures, audio,
and an offline AI assistant.

### What you can do

- **Learn the alphabet** — all 29 letters with audio, mark each as learned
- **Learn words** — illustrated vocabulary across animals, birds, fish,
  body parts, clothes, colors, numbers, and more
- **Practice reading** — sentences built from the words you've learned,
  with audio playback
- **Take quizzes** — test what you've learned with live scores and
  celebrations
- **Daily 5** — a short spaced-repetition session that brings back words
  you're about to forget
- **Ask Anywaa AI** — an offline assistant that translates between English
  and Dha Anywaa, gives pronunciations and example sentences, explains
  numbers, and shares cultural facts. Everything runs on your device; no
  internet needed
- **Read the Bible** — the full Bible (66 books) in Anywaa with English,
  search, night mode, and adjustable text size
- **Use the dictionary** — over 3,400 words with pronunciation guides and
  examples

The app keeps track of your progress with streaks, XP, and levels, and can
send you daily reminders so you never miss a lesson. It is completely free
and works offline.

Best wishes!
